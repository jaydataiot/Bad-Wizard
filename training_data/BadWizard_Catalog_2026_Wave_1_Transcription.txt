PAGES 5-6 - DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
FROM SPARK OF AN IDEA TO UNFORGETTABLE EXPERIENCE

OUR PROMISE
Every game we create follows a relentless process of imagination, iteration, and integrity.
We don't rush great games. We forge them.
Bold ideas. Thoughtful design. Unforgettable experiences.

PHASE 1 - IDEA
Goal: Find the spark. Explore themes, mechanics, and experiences worth building.
Deliverables: Concept Document; Theme & Mechanics Outline; Initial Mood Board.
Typical Timeline: 1-2 weeks.

PHASE 2 - PROTOTYPE
Goal: Build it rough. Test the core mechanics and experience in the simplest form.
Deliverables: Rough Prototype; Basic Rule Set; Playtest Notes.
Typical Timeline: 2-6 weeks.

PHASE 3 - INTERNAL PLAYTESTING
Goal: Test, break, and improve. Refine gameplay, balance, and fun factor.
Deliverables: Revised Prototype; Updated Rules; Internal Playtest Report.
Typical Timeline: 4-12 weeks.

PHASE 4 - INVITE-ONLY PUBLIC TESTING
Goal: Bring in trusted players. Gather diverse feedback in a controlled environment.
Deliverables: Public Playtest Build; Feedback Surveys; Playtest Summary.
Typical Timeline: 6-10 weeks.

PHASE 5 - MARKET ANALYSIS
Goal: Evaluate viability, audience fit, and market potential. Decide next steps.
Deliverables: Market Research Report; Cost Analysis; Go/No-Go Decision.
Typical Timeline: 2-4 weeks.

PHASE 6 - CROWDFUNDING
Goal: Share the vision. Build community and secure the resources to launch.
Deliverables: Campaign Page; Promotional Assets; Backer Community.
Typical Timeline: 30-45 days.

PHASE 7 - PRODUCTION
Goal: Manufacture with care. Ensure quality, components, and deadlines.
Deliverables: Final Game Files; Component Production; Quality Assurance.
Typical Timeline: 8-16 weeks.

PHASE 8 - RETAIL RELEASE
Goal: Deliver the game to players around the world. The adventure begins.
Deliverables: Finished Games; Rulebooks; Distribution to Retailers.
Typical Timeline: 2-4 weeks.

PAGE 7 - PUBLIC TESTING PROGRAM
PUBLIC TESTING PROGRAM
HELP SHAPE THE NEXT GENERATION OF BAD WIZARD GAMES

Our games are built with the community, not just for the community. The Public Testing Program exists to bring passionate, thoughtful players into our creative process early - when their feedback can have the greatest impact. Together, we make the game better.

APPLICATION FLOW
1. APPLICATION SUBMITTED - You complete the application on our website.
2. DEVELOPER REVIEW - Our team reviews your application and gaming background.
3. ACCEPTED / DECLINED - You'll be notified of our decision. We appreciate all interest.
4. INVITATION SENT - Accepted testers receive an official invitation to participate.
5. NDA - You review and sign our Non-Disclosure Agreement to protect the game and our community.
6. TESTING - You play the game, explore, break it, and experience everything it has to offer.
7. FEEDBACK - You share honest, constructive feedback through our feedback portal and surveys.
8. POSSIBLE CREDITS IN RULEBOOK - Exceptional testers may be credited in the final rulebook.

INVITATION ONLY
The Public Testing Program is invitation only to protect the integrity of our projects and our community. We carefully select testers who demonstrate a history of thoughtful play, constructive feedback, and respect for confidentiality. It's not about being the biggest fan - it's about being the right fit to help build something amazing.

TESTER EXPECTATIONS
- Play the game as intended - and try to break it.
- Test a variety of strategies and player counts.
- Take notes and share your experiences.
- Be respectful, thoughtful, and constructive.
- Meet deadlines and stay engaged.

FEEDBACK PROCESS
Feedback is submitted through our online portal. You'll find guided questions along with open-ended fields to share your thoughts in your own words.
- Share what you love, what confuses you, and what feels unbalanced or broken.
- Suggest improvements and new ideas.
- Rate key areas: fun, clarity, balance, theme, and replayability.
- Participate in follow-up discussions when needed.

PROTOTYPE CONFIDENTIALITY
Our prototypes are sacred. What you see, play, and learn is not for public discussion.
- Sign and honor the NDA.
- Do not share components, files, or images.
- Do not discuss the game online or offline outside the program.
- Respect the hard work of our team and the trust we place in you.

COMMUNITY RECOGNITION
- Credits in the rulebook for exceptional testers.
- Early access to future games and updates.
- Special roles in our community.
- Invitations to exclusive playtest events.

THE BEST GAMES ARE BUILT TOGETHER. - JEFFREY A. YOUNG

PAGE 9 - CULT LEADER
CULT LEADER
POWER. FAITH. CONTROL. IT'S GOOD TO BE FOLLOWED.

EXPANDED LORE
In a world where truth is flexible and belief is currency, you are the architect of a movement. As a Cult Leader, you will gather devoted followers, spread your doctrine, outmaneuver rival leaders, and seize control of the masses.

But power is never free. Influence brings scrutiny. Rivals plot in secret. Faith can turn... and empires built on lies are always one crisis away from collapse.

How will your legacy be remembered?

CORE MECHANICS
FOLLOWER ACQUISITION - Recruit followers to grow your cult and expand your influence.
DOCTRINE DEVELOPMENT - Craft powerful doctrines to shape beliefs and unlock abilities.
RESOURCE MANAGEMENT - Manage Faith, Influence, and Resources to fuel your growth.
RIVALRY & SABOTAGE - Undermine rival cults through plots, scandals, and deception.
RITUALS & EVENTS - Perform rituals to gain bonuses - but beware the consequences.
VICTORY CONDITIONS - Dominate through faith, control, or infamy. Multiple paths to victory.

2-5 PLAYERS. 60-120 MINUTES. AGES 14+.

FACTIONS
THE FAITHFUL - Zealous and unwavering. They win through pure faith and massive numbers. Strength in unity and devotion.
THE ILLUMINED - Seekers of hidden knowledge. They manipulate events and control minds from the shadows.
THE PROFITEERS - Mere belief isn't enough - they want wealth and power. They trade, bribe, and buy their empire.
THE HERETICS - Chaos incarnate. They thrive on disruption, rebellion, and turning the faithful against all.
THE ASCENDED - They believe they are beyond mortals. They seek transcendence - at any cost.

EXAMPLE CARDS
RALLY THE FAITHFUL - Gain 3 Followers. If you control a Temple, gain 2 extra Faith.
SECRET MEETING - Look at the top 3 cards of any player's deck. Choose 1 and discard the others.
DIVINE INSPIRATION - Draw 2 Doctrine cards. Choose 1 to keep, discard the other.
SCANDAL - Target opponent loses 2 Influence and must discard 1 random Follower.
GRAND RITUAL - Pay 3 Faith to gain 5 Influence. Draw 1 card.
