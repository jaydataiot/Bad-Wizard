================================================== OVERVIEW
==================================================

Bad Wizard Games is an independent tabletop game studio focused on
creating original board games that combine strong thematic storytelling,
memorable characters, strategic gameplay, dark humor, horror, satire,
and cinematic presentation. The company emphasizes highly immersive game
worlds where players become active participants in evolving stories
rather than simply moving pieces around a board.

The studio’s philosophy is built around one central belief:

“Every game should tell a story worth remembering.”

Rather than producing games that rely solely on mechanics, Bad Wizard
Games attempts to create complete entertainment experiences including:

• Rich lore • Distinct visual identity • Memorable characters • Strong
player interaction • Replayability • Expandable worlds • Digital
integration • AI-assisted companion experiences

================================================== FOUNDER
==================================================

Bad Wizard Games was founded by Jeffrey Young.

Jeffrey Young has a background spanning computer science, artificial
intelligence, machine learning, data science, predictive analytics,
cybersecurity research, software engineering, and university-level
teaching.

His professional experience combines technical engineering with creative
design. The studio represents the intersection of analytical thinking
and creative storytelling.

The goal of Bad Wizard Games is not simply to publish games, but to
build original intellectual properties capable of expanding into
multiple forms of entertainment including digital games, AI experiences,
online communities, companion applications, fiction, and educational
content.

================================================== COMPANY PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Bad Wizard Games believes that:

• Every game should feel like entering a new world. • Theme should
reinforce mechanics. • Player interaction creates memorable stories. •
Humor and horror can coexist. • Rules should encourage creativity. •
Every play session should create unique moments. • Players should feel
agency over the narrative. • High production quality enhances immersion.
• Community feedback improves every game.

================================================== DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The studio designs games around several principles:

1.  Strong Theme First Mechanics are chosen to reinforce the story
    rather than existing independently.

2.  Meaningful Choices Players should rarely have obvious decisions.

3.  High Replayability Randomized setups, evolving objectives,
    asymmetric powers, and player interaction create different
    experiences every session.

4.  Social Interaction Most games encourage negotiation, bluffing,
    alliances, sabotage, or cooperation.

5.  Accessibility Rules should be approachable while allowing strategic
    depth.

================================================== CURRENT GAME CATALOG
==================================================

FIRST WAVE

Cult Leader A strategic dark satire where players compete to build the
most influential cult through manipulation, recruitment, deception, and
resource management.

Double Wide A humorous trailer-park strategy game featuring outrageous
characters, neighborhood politics, questionable life choices, and
constantly escalating chaos.

Pixel Cooperative A cooperative city-building game where players work
together to construct and defend a growing pixel-inspired civilization.

Blood Suckers Set in the year 2148 after humanity develops advanced
anti-vampire technologies. Players control hidden vampire factions
attempting to survive extinction while infiltrating governments and
corporations.

F.A.F.O. A 1970s comic-book inspired gang warfare game set in Cotton
City. Players control rival syndicates until each boss is eliminated by
the narrative, forcing shifting allegiances throughout the campaign.

10.31.84 A horror experience containing multiple interconnected stories.
Clever players may discover a hidden third story.

Slasher A cinematic horror survival game inspired by classic slasher
films where every decision may determine who survives the night.

Grab-A-Ghost An adult horror game centered around Watson Manor. The manor has waited a century for someone to knock on its door. Enter a living house where every room hides a new nightmare, every clue may be a lie, and every investigation reveals a different truth. Work together to survive impossible horrors—but remember, not everyone who escapes leaves the manor unchanged.

The Judge’s Eye A mystery-themed investigative experience centered
around solving increasingly dangerous cases.
