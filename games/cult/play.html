How to Play — Cult Leader

Build followers, gather resources (Faith, Wealth, Fear, Secrecy), raise your Temple, manage Suspicion, and survive Events.

Setup

  • Pick an Archetype & a Doctrine.
  • Start with 5 Followers, F=2, W=2, R=1, S=1; place a free Altar at Round 1.
  • Suspicion starts at 0.

Round Flow

  1. Recruitment: Preach (F vs TN7), Bribe (−2W), or Intimidate (R vs TN6; +1 Susp).
  2. Resource: Each Follower does 1 task: Pray (+F), Work (+W), Guard (+R), Hide (−Susp).
  3. Construction: Build/upgrade Temple (2→4→6→8 W). Suspicion +1.
  4. Event: Draw & resolve a random Event.

Temple Benefits

  • Altar: +1 Faith
  • Shrine: +1 Secrecy
  • Sanctuary: +2 Faith
  • Grand Temple: +3 Faith & +2 Wealth each Round

Suspicion & Final Raid

When Suspicion hits max, roll Secrecy vs TN10. Fail = collapse. Survive and continue until victory.

Win Conditions

  • Dominance: Hold a Grand Temple for 3 Rounds.
  • Last Cult Standing.
  • Final Raid survivor with highest Faith (variant).
