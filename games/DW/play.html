The Setup

Welcome to Graceland Mobile Estates

Life is never simple in Graceland Mobile Estates. Bobbi-Sue promised Reverend Dolla Bill that she would bring T-Roy to church, but T-Roy refuses to move until a collection of neighborhood problems has been solved.

Explore the park, question the neighbors, gather clues, and find the items needed to change T-Roy's mind—while staying one step ahead of Big-E and his demand for back lot rent.

The Game Loop

Search. Talk. Collect. Run.

🗺 Explore the Park

Search trailers, yards, paths, and back lots for useful objects and overlooked secrets.

💬 Question the Neighbors

Every resident knows part of the story. Conversations reveal clues and point Bobbi-Sue toward her next objective.

🧰 Find the Required Items

Track down the objects T-Roy demands before he will consider leaving the trailer and going to church.

💵 Protect Your Cash

Big-E takes $20 every time he catches Bobbi-Sue. When her money reaches $0, the game is over.

🏃 Avoid Big-E

Watch his route, change direction quickly, and keep moving while you complete objectives around the neighborhood.

⛪ Get T-Roy to Church

Collect everything, finish the neighborhood errands, convince T-Roy, and complete Bobbi-Sue's promise to Reverend Dolla Bill.
