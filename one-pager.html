01 · The studio

Games that reveal the people playing them.

Founded in 2025 by Jeffrey Young—a data scientist, AI engineer, machine learning engineer, and lifelong game enthusiast—Bad Wizard Games is an independent tabletop studio built around a simple belief: story is not decoration; it is the engine of play. The most memorable games create a world, put people inside it, and reveal how they react when the choices get strange.

We create dark, satirical, and culturally observant games that turn social tension, flawed decisions, and sharp humor into shared experiences. Our work blends narrative design, systems thinking, and modern creative technology to produce games that are easy to enter, hard to predict, and even harder to stop talking about.

Story is the system

Every mechanic earns its place by deepening the premise or sharpening a choice.

Reaction is gameplay

The best moments emerge from how players read, challenge, and surprise one another.

Humor has teeth

Our games use satire and cultural observation to create laughter with an aftertaste.
