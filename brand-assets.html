06 · Usage

Keep the magic intact.

A few simple rules help every appearance feel unmistakably Bad Wizard.

✓

Give it breathing room

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the mark’s width.

✓

Favor strong contrast

Place the mark on solid or visually quiet backgrounds where every detail remains clear.

×

Don’t redraw or distort

Never stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or rebuild the logo from separate elements.

×

Don’t add effects

Avoid unapproved gradients, glows, shadows, bevels, and other decorative treatments.
