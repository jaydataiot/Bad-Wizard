Double Wide

A tactical, dark-humor community builder set in Lamb Chop, Mississippi. Balance schemes and neighborly chaos as you claw your way to local legend.

2–6 Players45–75 MinutesAges 14+Strategy / Satire

Overview

Double Wide is part engine-builder, part negotiation game where every neighbor has a backstory and every favor has strings attached. Expand your lot, hustle resources, trigger Events, and navigate the trailer-park pecking order without landing on the wrong side of a grudge.

Expect quick turns, lively table talk, risky deals, and surprise combinations as players improve their homes, trade favors, and use cards that bend the rules—sometimes to their benefit and sometimes to a neighbor’s.

The Objective

Earn the most Reputation by upgrading your lot, completing favors, helping or outmaneuvering neighbors, and pulling off bold Schemes.

The player with the highest Reputation at the end of the game becomes the undisputed local legend of Lamb Chop.

Game Experience

  • Build and upgrade your trailer lot
  • Collect cash, supplies, and useful favors
  • Negotiate deals with unpredictable neighbors
  • Play Schemes and survive sudden Mishaps
  • Compete for status in the neighborhood

How a Turn Works

Collect Income

Gain cash, supplies, and benefits produced by your lot, upgrades, and active neighbor relationships.

Take Actions

Upgrade your property, complete a Favor, trade resources, play a Scheme, or interact with a neighbor.

Resolve Chaos

Apply card effects, Mishaps, grudges, Event consequences, and any deals made around the table.

Update Reputation

Score completed goals, adjust your neighborhood standing, and prepare for the next turn.

Lots & Resources

Your lot is the heart of your engine. Improvements can generate cash, supplies, or Reputation, while certain combinations unlock stronger effects. Smart players balance long-term upgrades against short-term opportunities.

Cash pays for improvements and deals, while resources and Favor cards provide flexibility when the neighborhood turns against you.

Schemes, Favors & Mishaps

Schemes let players manipulate resources, bend rules, or disrupt rivals. Favors reward cooperation but often create obligations. Mishaps introduce sudden setbacks, arguments, accidents, and other chaotic events that can completely reshape the table.

What’s in the Box

  • 1 modular Neighborhood board and player mats
  • 120+ Resource and Cash tokens
  • 70 Scheme, Favor, and Mishap cards
  • Event deck and Character tiles
  • Dice, tracker markers, and Rulebook

Replayability

Changing Character combinations, modular neighborhood layouts, shuffled Events, varied Schemes, and unpredictable player deals make every session different. Players can focus on property development, social influence, economic control, or high-risk disruption.

Game Modes & Variants

  • Standard Competitive: Finish with the most Reputation.
  • Low Heat: Remove harsher Mishaps and use gentler Event cards.
  • Advanced Neighborhood: Add stronger grudges, chained events, and tighter resources.
  • Team Variant: Neighbors form temporary partnerships while still tracking individual Reputation.

Content Guidance

Double Wide uses satire and dark humor involving neighborhood conflict, poverty, questionable decisions, grudges, and chaotic mishaps. Use the optional Low Heat variant if your group prefers gentler events and less aggressive player interaction.
