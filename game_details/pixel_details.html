PIXEL CO-OP

A cooperative planetary city-building board game where every player has a job, every resource matters, and no colony survives alone.

1–4 Players75–120 MinutesAges 12+Cooperative Strategy

Overview

In PIXEL CO-OP, players land on a hostile world with only a settlement site, a limited supply cache, and a direct line to the Stellar Cooperative. Working together, the team must build power, air, water, food, mining, housing, transportation, and civic districts until the outpost becomes a livable city.

Each player controls a specialist role with unique abilities. The colony grows through shared planning, negotiated resource use, district adjacency bonuses, research upgrades, rover exploration, and branching Cooperative contracts. Once the city meets its launch conditions, the team travels to the next planet, where new terrain and harsher rules change the puzzle.

The Objective

The team wins a planet by reaching its City-Ready requirements: maintain a stable population, keep essential resources above crisis levels, complete required districts, satisfy Cooperative objectives, and construct the Launch Complex.

A full campaign victory requires successfully establishing cities across all ten planets.

Game Experience

  • Plan every turn as a cooperative team
  • Build and optimize planetary districts
  • Manage air, water, food, power, metal, and credits
  • Explore terrain with specialized rovers
  • Advance through a ten-planet campaign

How a Round Works

Cooperative Planning

Players discuss priorities, assign actions, and determine what the colony needs most.

Build & Upgrade

Spend metal and credits to place structures, create districts, or improve existing buildings.

Produce & Consume

Buildings generate resources while settlers consume air, water, food, and power.

Resolve the Planet

Apply hazards, contracts, dilemmas, rover discoveries, and planet-specific events.

Player Roles

  • Engineer: Reduces upgrade costs, improves energy efficiency, and accelerates blueprint development.
  • Prospector: Increases mining yield, strengthens rover surveys, and finds valuable terrain opportunities.
  • Quartermaster: Negotiates cheaper imports, improves contract rewards, and can purchase additional settlers.
  • Fourth Specialist: Included through the role-board system for alternate team composition and replayability.

Resources & Districts

Settlers depend on a balanced supply of air, water, food, and power. Metal and credits fund construction, upgrades, imports, and Cooperative purchases.

Placing compatible buildings next to one another creates district adjacency bonuses, making efficient city layout just as important as raw production.

Planet Campaign

The campaign begins on the dust-red world Red Dune-7, then expands to ice, jungle, basalt, ocean, radiation, storm, and deep-space worlds. Each planet changes terrain colors, resource availability, building rules, hazards, and contract pressure.

Campaign progression unlocks blueprints, stronger rovers, advanced districts, improved Cooperative reputation, and permanent team bonuses.

Replayability & Modes

  • Campaign: Build cities across ten linked planets.
  • Single Planet: Play any unlocked scenario as a standalone challenge.
  • Solo: Control multiple specialists and manage the colony alone.
  • Hard Mode: Add stronger hazards, tighter resources, and harsher failure conditions.

Content Guidance

The game focuses on survival pressure, resource shortages, colony collapse, environmental hazards, and difficult cooperative decisions. The tone is science-fictional rather than graphic, but failed colonies and population loss may be stressful for younger players.
