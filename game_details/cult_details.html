Cult Leader

A strategic game of persuasion, secrecy, and spectacle. Manage followers, outmaneuver rivals, and survive scandals in a satirical contest for power and charisma.

2–5 Players60–90 MinutesAges 14+Strategy / Satire

Overview

In Cult Leader, each player runs a burgeoning movement, juggling resources such as Followers, Secrecy, and Wealth. Turns mix tactical choices with fast table talk: build Temples, stage Spectacles, spread Rumors, and weather unpredictable Events such as Government Raids or Miracle Windfalls.

It is competitive, clever, and loaded with opportunities for dramatic reversals. The game is deliberately satirical, rewarding players who can balance expansion, reputation, and risk without allowing their movement to collapse under public scrutiny.

The Objective

Accrue the most Devotion by growing your movement, completing Spectacles, building influence, and surviving public scandals. Timing your risks—and reading your opponents—is everything.

The player with the highest Devotion at the end of the game becomes the dominant Cult Leader.

Game Experience

  • Recruit and manage Followers
  • Balance Wealth, Secrecy, and public attention
  • Construct Temples and stage Spectacles
  • Spread Rumors and disrupt rival movements
  • React to Raids, Scandals, Miracles, and Betrayals

How a Turn Works

Gather Resources

Collect income, Followers, and other benefits generated by your movement and its structures.

Take Actions

Recruit Followers, build a Temple, play a Scheme, spread a Rumor, or prepare a Spectacle.

Resolve Effects

Apply card effects, rival interference, scandals, and any triggered Event consequences.

Check Devotion

Score completed goals, update your resources, and prepare for the next player’s turn.

Followers & Resources

Followers expand your movement and help generate Devotion, while Wealth funds buildings, Schemes, and Spectacles. Secrecy protects you from exposure and makes it harder for rivals or Events to dismantle your plans.

Growing too quickly can leave you vulnerable, while playing too cautiously may allow another leader to dominate the table.

Schemes & Spectacles

Scheme cards create tactical opportunities, including recruitment boosts, resource manipulation, rumors, and attacks on rival leaders. Spectacles are larger public actions that can generate major Devotion but may also draw attention or trigger dangerous consequences.

What’s in the Box

  • 1 Game Board and 5 Player Boards
  • 120+ Followers and Resource tokens
  • 60 Event cards featuring Raids, Scandals, Miracles, Betrayals, and Tragedies
  • 30 Scheme cards and 20 Spectacle tiles
  • Dice, Reference cards, and Rulebook

Replayability

Shuffled Events, varied Scheme combinations, different Spectacle opportunities, changing rival strategies, and unpredictable resource pressures make each session play differently. Players can pursue aggressive recruitment, economic control, secrecy, or high-risk public spectacle as their primary path to victory.

Game Modes & Variants

  • Standard Competitive: Build the strongest movement and finish with the most Devotion.
  • Low Heat: Remove selected Event cards and soften several effects for a lighter game.
  • Advanced Play: Use more disruptive Schemes and harsher Event combinations.
  • Team Variant: Leaders form temporary alliances while retaining individual victory conditions.

Content Guidance

This game uses satire and dark humor around themes of manipulation, belief, power, and media spectacle. If your group prefers a lighter tone, use the optional Low Heat variant, which removes selected Event cards and softens several effects.
