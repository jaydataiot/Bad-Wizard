A Halloween Murder Mystery

10.31.84

Three trick-or-treaters were murdered on Halloween night, 1984. Search the neighborhood, question the suspects, uncover hidden motives, and solve the whodunit before the killer disappears into the darkness.

2–6 Players60–120 MinutesAges 16+Mystery / Deduction

Overview

10.31.84 is a competitive deduction and investigation game set in a quiet American neighborhood on Halloween night. Players take on the roles of investigators trying to reconstruct the final hours of three young victims. Every location may hide evidence, every witness may be lying, and every suspect has something to conceal.

Explore the neighborhood, collect physical evidence, compare witness statements, establish timelines, and eliminate impossible theories. The solution changes from game to game, so the murderer, motive, method, and critical evidence are never guaranteed to be the same.

The Objective

Be the first investigator to correctly identify:

  • The murderer
  • The motive
  • The murder weapon or method
  • The location of the decisive crime

A correct accusation wins the game. A false accusation removes that player from making another final accusation, though they may remain involved as an observer or limited investigator depending on the chosen rules variant.

Game Experience

  • Explore a modular 1984 neighborhood
  • Interview witnesses and confront suspects
  • Build a timeline from conflicting clues
  • Use deduction to expose false leads
  • Race rival investigators to the truth

How a Turn Works

Move

Travel to a nearby house, street, yard, wooded area, or other neighborhood location.

Investigate

Search the location, examine an object, question a witness, or follow a lead.

Resolve

Draw or reveal the appropriate clue, event, or encounter and apply its effect.

Record Evidence

Update your case notes, eliminate possibilities, and prepare your theory.

Clues & Investigation

Clues are divided into categories such as physical evidence, witness testimony, timelines, suspect behavior, and location evidence. Some clues are reliable, some are incomplete, and others may be deliberate misdirection.

Players must decide which evidence fits together and which details cannot all be true at the same time.

Suspects & Motives

Each suspect has a public background, a private secret, a possible motive, and a relationship to the victims or the neighborhood. As evidence is revealed, players uncover grudges, hidden meetings, family conflicts, rivalries, and rumors from the night of the murders.

What’s in the Box

  • 1 illustrated neighborhood game board
  • 6 investigator pawns and player reference cards
  • Suspect, witness, victim, and location cards
  • Evidence, event, rumor, and encounter decks
  • Case solution envelopes or solution card sets
  • Investigation markers, timeline tokens, and clue tokens
  • Investigator note sheets
  • Rulebook and case setup guide

Replayability

Variable suspect combinations, shuffled evidence, alternate motives, modular clue chains, and multiple possible solutions make every investigation different. Optional advanced rules add secret objectives, unreliable witnesses, time pressure, and evidence that can be hidden or traded.

Game Modes

  • Competitive: First correct accusation wins.
  • Cooperative: Work together to solve the case before time runs out.
  • Solo: Investigate against a limited turn clock and score your performance.
  • Story Mode: Play linked cases with persistent narrative consequences.

Content Guidance

This game contains fictional murder, child victims, implied violence, disturbing crime-scene themes, and dark Halloween imagery. It is intended for mature players. Groups may remove graphic clue cards or use a lighter descriptive variant when preferred.
