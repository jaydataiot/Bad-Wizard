Grab-a-Ghost

A fast, competitive adult horror game in which paranormal investigators explore a haunted location, weaken dangerous spirits, spring unstable traps, and sabotage one another before the dead turn the hunters into prey.

2–6 Players30–45 MinutesAges 18+Horror / Take-That

Overview

In Grab-a-Ghost, each player controls a paranormal investigator trapped inside a violently haunted location. Investigators move from room to room, search for spectral activity, collect cursed equipment, weaken hostile entities, and attempt to seal ghosts inside unreliable trap devices.

Capturing spirits earns points, but every investigation increases the danger. Rival players can steal equipment, interfere with traps, redirect hauntings, release captured ghosts, and force opponents into rooms they would rather avoid. The game mixes push-your-luck exploration, tactical card play, dice-based trapping, and aggressive player interaction.

The Objective

Capture the highest total value of ghosts before the investigation ends. Common spirits provide reliable points, while dangerous entities and boss ghosts are harder to contain but can dramatically change the final score.

The investigator with the most captured ghost points wins. If only one investigator remains alive, that survivor wins immediately—regardless of the score.

Game Experience

  • Explore a shifting haunted location
  • Search rooms for ghosts, items, and cursed events
  • Weaken spirits before attempting a capture
  • Manage sanity, injuries, and limited equipment
  • Sabotage rival investigators and steal opportunities
  • Risk stronger hauntings for more valuable ghosts

How a Turn Works

Resolve the Haunting

Apply any ghost, room, possession, or ongoing curse effects affecting your investigator at the beginning of the turn.

Take Up to Three Actions

Move, search, use an item, trade with another investigator, weaken a ghost, interfere with a rival, or attempt to activate your trap.

Attempt a Capture

If a weakened ghost is in your location, spend an action to roll the trap die. Success seals the ghost in your device; failure may trigger an attack or release an additional haunting.

Draw the Location Event

End the turn by resolving the room or event effect, then pass play clockwise unless a card changes the turn order.

Ghosts & Hauntings

Ghost cards describe the spirit’s strength, behavior, point value, weakness, and capture condition. Some entities attack immediately, some stalk the investigator who disturbed them, and others remain dormant until a specific event awakens them.

Boss ghosts are exceptionally dangerous. They may possess investigators, disable traps, lock rooms, or release previously captured spirits back into play.

Traps & Ectoplasm

Most ghosts must be weakened before they can be captured. Investigators use equipment, environmental effects, and Ectoplasm tokens to reduce a ghost’s strength or satisfy its special weakness.

When a capture succeeds, place the ghost beneath your Trap Device and collect any listed reward. An overloaded or damaged trap may malfunction, allowing every stored spirit to escape at once.

Sanity & Damage

Ghost attacks, cursed rooms, and forbidden items can reduce an investigator’s Sanity or cause physical Damage. Low Sanity makes possession and panic effects more likely, while accumulated Damage limits movement and equipment use.

At zero Sanity, an investigator becomes temporarily haunted and may be forced to harm the group. An investigator who suffers a fatal result is removed from the game and may leave behind equipment—or a new ghost.

Items & Dirty Tricks

Item cards include scanners, cameras, ritual tools, improvised weapons, protective charms, batteries, and suspicious homemade equipment. Many provide legitimate help; others are unstable, cursed, or designed primarily to ruin another investigator’s turn.

Take-that cards can swap traps, redirect attacks, steal Ectoplasm, lock doors, trigger false readings, or release a rival’s most valuable ghost.

  • Trap die, reference cards, first-player marker, and rulebook

Ending the Game

The investigation ends when the Ghost deck is empty, the final boss haunting is resolved, or all investigators except one have been eliminated. Players total the values of the ghosts still secured inside their traps and apply any endgame bonuses or penalties.

Escaped ghosts do not score. Ties are broken by remaining Sanity, then by unused Ectoplasm.

Game Modes & Variants

  • Competitive Hunt: Every investigator competes for the highest-value collection.
  • Last One Breathing: Elimination and survival matter more than points.
  • Cooperative Exorcism: Players combine captured values to defeat a final boss ghost.
  • After Midnight: Add harsher events, trap malfunctions, and adult-content cards.

Replayability

Randomized room layouts, changing ghost combinations, unique investigator abilities, unpredictable item draws, and escalating hauntings make every investigation different. Players may focus on efficient trapping, aggressive sabotage, equipment control, survival, or high-risk boss hunting.

Content Guidance

Grab-a-Ghost is intended for adults and contains horror imagery, death, possession, occult rituals, dark humor, crude language, player sabotage, and references to violence. Groups wanting a lighter experience can remove the marked After Midnight cards and use the cooperative variant.
